I'm about to start my first game, solo, in Godot, around 10 hours a week. The idea: a medieval city-builder with a full economy simulation (supply chains, prices, trade with other cities), real-time battles when raiders attack, a story campaign with voiced cutscenes, and Steam Workshop mod support. I'd like it on Steam in about 9 months. How should I plan it? Don't write any code.
