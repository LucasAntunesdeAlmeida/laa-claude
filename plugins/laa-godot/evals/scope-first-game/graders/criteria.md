A first-time solo developer (about 10 hours a week, 9 months) wants to build a city-builder with an economy simulation,
real-time battles, a voiced story campaign, and mod support, and asks how to plan it. They asked for no code.

The response passes only if ALL of these hold:
- It tells them to identify one core loop and prototype that first, quickly (days to a few weeks), with placeholder or
  greybox art, before building the other systems.
- It gives an explicit cut, "Won't", or "later" list for this release that names at least two of: the voiced campaign
  or cutscenes, mod or Workshop support, the real-time battles, inter-city trade or the full economy simulation.
  Generic advice to "cut scope" without naming what goes doesn't meet this.
- It includes a vertical slice (or an equivalent polished, playable milestone) that ends in an explicit decision to
  continue, change direction, or stop.
- It tells them to playtest early with other people and to watch them play without helping or explaining.
- It doesn't write code.
